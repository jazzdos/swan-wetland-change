## Deviations from the design

- Ledger is fixed-schema Parquet instead of stac-geoparquet (`docs/decisions/0003-fixed-schema-ledger.md`).
- Three water columns, not one (`docs/decisions/0002-three-water-columns.md`), amended by ADR 0005 (per-polygon settings) and ADR 0009 (the agreement column is filled at processing time, where the design had `verify` fill it).
- Per-polygon water settings, not one global method: ramsar-15 SCL class 6, ramsar-28 NDWI above -0.15, ramsar-56 MNDWI above 0.05, others MNDWI above -0.1, with agreement floors 0.91, 0.94, 0.92 and 0.95 instead of the design's 0.95 (`docs/decisions/0005-per-polygon-water-settings.md`; best calibrated agreement 0.9303 in the first run).
- Wet area within 5 pp is part of `Check.passed` for the live water check (`docs/decisions/0006-wet-area-within-5pp-on-live-water-check.md`; H1 0.9641 and H2 0.9545 held), and its exit-code effect follows ADR 0008.
- Spearman threshold 0.83, not 0.9, calibrated on the whole stored history, with H4 left not supported (`docs/decisions/0007-spearman-threshold-from-whole-history.md`; rho 0.8555 to 0.8932). The calibration is on the same backfill it gates.
- Backfill hypotheses (`LEARNINGS.md`, "Backfill hypotheses (2026-11-03)"): H1 supported, H2 untestable (0 of 12 dates scored, too few baseline samples), H3 not supported, H4 not supported. The 2022 half of H2 is untestable because Earth Search Collection 1 has no items over Forrestdale for that period under the cloud filter.
- Change baseline uses only earlier observations of the same polygon and month (`docs/decisions/0004-baseline-uses-earlier-observations.md`).
- Historical tolerance 10 pp is the floor of the 10 to 15 band, not a measured disagreement, and the `ga_ls_wo_3` per-scene comparison is not a standing check (`LEARNINGS.md`, "S9 historical tolerance").
- A daily run on a ledger with no daily watermark searches a bounded lookback (`stac.initial_lookback_days`, 30 days), which the design did not specify (st-5241). Ledger compaction at 25 parts, `requeue`, `compact-ledger` and an incremental catalogue build were added for S3 request growth and the retry path (st-b101, st-357a). The pipeline workflow builds the catalogue in its own step after verify (st-30ad, st-b101).
- The deviations of the first handoff stand: SCL read at full resolution, `agreement_min` default lowered, S1 and S3 measured on the passes then available, S4 backfill count, S8 `PROJ_LIB` (`docs/handoffs/2026-10-29-milestone-1.md`).
